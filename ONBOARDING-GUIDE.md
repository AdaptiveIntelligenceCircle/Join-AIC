# Contributor Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Adaptive Intelligence Circle.

## Who We Are
We are building an independent, ethical AI protocol that prioritizes long-term human autonomy over short-term optimization.

## Our Core Principles
- Zero-Donation (no funding with strings)
- Third Path Absolute (independent from Big Tech and governments)
- Ethical-from-Kernel (ethics must be part of the foundation, not an add-on)

## How to Contribute

**Step 1:** Read our core documents
- ZERO-DONATION-POLICY.md
- THIRD-PATH-PRINCIPLES.md
- GOVERNANCE.md

**Step 2:** Choose your level
- Observer → read & give feedback
- Contributor → submit PRs, fix issues, write docs
- Active Builder → consistent contribution
- Trusted Admin / TSC → long-term stewardship

**Step 3:** Open an Issue or Discussion with what you want to work on.

We value **quality and alignment** over quantity. Even small, thoughtful contributions are welcome.

Thank you for considering joining us.
